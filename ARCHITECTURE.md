# ArcCivs settlement contract

Players found one settlement, choose a curated imported building style, place
and preview a town hall site, welcome gradual pioneers, assign a hut builder, build production and housing,
recruit residents, and upgrade buildings/town hall through three levels.
The settlement survives reload/restart. Owner-managed membership grants city
management. Owners and members may remodel their structures, including construction
footprints at every phase; unrelated players and environmental changes remain guarded.

## Autonomous village variation

`Settlement.autonomy != null` is a separate immutable mode, created only by an
administrator. Its real owner remains the protection and chunk permission actor;
it has no personal membership index entries, so the owner can retain a personal
city. Both local and SQL stores fence owner/mode changes. SQL migration 3 keeps
autonomous deletion receipts in a separate table so older backends cannot consume
a player's personal one-time founding reserve after an autonomous deletion. Do not downgrade a backend that has
created autonomous snapshots to a version without this mode.

`CivsAutonomy` chooses one paid goal from nine building types, with three
HOUSE sites (eleven service buildings), ending at level two. It uses aggregate `Stock` and always has `inventory == null`; migration
to native item stores excludes this mode. Complete schematic details are covered
by a one-time wood/stone/food price at `reserveBuild`. Producers bypass natural
world work and exact inputs, but retain the normal NPC journey, work duration,
depot arrival check and unique cycle receipt before stock is credited. No elapsed
time, teleport recovery or forecast alone credits a delivery. This is deliberately
abstract resource production, not evidence of actual crop/ore harvesting.
Autonomous producers walk between the completed workplace entrance and the depot
entrance, including a founding SITE hall whose authored barrel is above unfinished
ground. The final receipt rechecks the observed production building, cycle, mode,
world, delivery phase and safe native endpoint after waiting for another city save.
An unrelated stock revision does not invalidate a journey. Physical-item settlements
retain their barrel reach and inventory receipt requirements.

The service submits the selected SITE or upgrade through the existing captured
schematic store, pending control receipt, queue, builder work credit and batch
placement journal. There is one admitted decision per city; callbacks recheck
the current mode, goal, pause, activity and construction state. Pausing stops new
decisions, producer receipts, active population minutes and native paste progress;
it preserves accepted orders and their paid stock. Food and post-seed recruitment
consume abstract food in the same durable city revision. No offline catch-up is
performed, and ordinary health/death and global NPC/chunk limits still apply.

`CivsAutonomousPlacement` tries at most 64 centre-facing candidates, reserves
future building bounds, field space and entrances, and admits at most two searches.
`CivsAutonomousTerraform` ranks median-height terraces and nearby levels by their
earthwork cost, then plans a graded connection from an existing entrance. A selected
site cuts natural ground, fills voids above a solid foundation and clears approach
headroom. A bounded 24-block downward tree scan separates soil height from crown
height. Nonpersistent foliage and logs below that foliage may be cleared; bare
log structures and persistent player foliage are refused. The exact journal keeps
that leaf evidence for revalidation and restart replay without producing drops.
Route steps move one horizontal block and at most one vertical block;
the starting support and standing space remain unchanged. Three-block shoulders
are prepared where natural terrain permits. The existing navigation solver checks
native collision snapshots with the proposed terrain overlaid before admission.
This proves a route on the prepared ground, rather than requiring it to exist
through the unmodified hill. A pinned legacy town hall SITE keeps its anchor and
receives the same preparation when activated; an active building upgrade retains
its completed footprint.

Native geometry reads share `CivsWorld`'s existing 2,048-cell / 2 ms scan budget.
The height field is limited to 65,536 columns and a deterministic radius of at most
127 even when the city's radius grows. Each selected draft captures at most 131,072
cells and changes at most 32,768. Cut or fill is limited to 24 blocks relative to
the surface, with a further bounded 24-block search for foundation support. Route
search expands at most 32,768 nodes and retains at most 256 route positions; no
chunks are loaded or generated by planning. Water, bedrock, foreign structures,
containers, registered storage, other reserved footprints and fields, border or
Lands denial remain hard boundaries. An unsupported deep void or disconnected
protected region can still refuse a candidate. New sites and upgrade expansion
cells reject non-natural blocks; filled containers remain protected inside an
owned upgrade footprint too.
Final schematic capture uses the same natural-ground whitelist below its anchor,
including sand and gravel exposed beneath the prepared terrace; it does not permit
those materials above the site's ground limit or bypass liquids and tile entities.

`CivPlacementPlan.terrain` stores exact before/after earthwork cells separately
from the original schematic snapshot. Its deterministic builder batches precede
all schematic batches: the approach advances from its existing source, followed
by the terrace. Cuts run top-down and fill runs bottom-up. The saved batch cursor
and preparation workplace resume the same journal after interruption. Subsequent
schematic batches retain the proven exterior entrance as their workplace until the
whole building finishes, including when its authored control is above a future
interior floor. A native
write rechecks identity, permission, protected cells and exact before/after state.
Schematic before-images at overlapping cells refer to the completed earthwork
state. Terrain creates no block drops or cash reward; the fixed building price
and aggregate wood/stone/food production rates are unchanged. Source tests cover
uneven terrain and replay safety, and are separate from native-client visual
acceptance of a constructed village.

Completed building captures retain the executed terrain journal, including a SITE
recaptured before its first payment. Later roads preserve these earlier route
columns and entrance grades; their shoulders cannot bury them, and new footprints
cannot cover them. A bounded legacy repair derives exact inverse writes from
later retained journals that obstruct older completed approaches. It runs before
growth even with empty stock, changes at most 512 cells and one block per tick,
and skips changed, occupied or protected cells. It neither grants resources nor
counts as resident construction; `autonomous_access_repaired` reports its counts.

`civs debug autonomy [city UUID]` reads cached goal, stock, retry and placement
progress and the last bounded placement result without starting work. `autonomous_goal_selected`,
`autonomous_goal_queued`, `autonomous_resource_delivered` and bounded
`autonomous_development_stalled` WARNs expose actual accepted transitions.
The earlier [design proposal](docs/autonomous-settlement-design.md) is historical;
its player-supplied exact-item economy is not this implemented variation.

## Shared settlement runtime

Owners with `arccivs.testing` can persist three per-settlement testing switches in
the management menu. Chunk loading defaults on for legacy cities and still requires
`arccivs.chunkload`; immortality and automatic construction supplies default off.
The latter two require the owner's current world permission through the existing
15-second refresh / 30-second allowance cache. Immortality clamps accepted damage
to one remaining health; it does not resurrect dead residents. Automatic supplies
cover only the deficit of the current captured construction row after local hut
stock and builder bags, fit real builder bag slots, and commit through the same
inventory journal. They are explicit administrative item issuance, not ordinary
production. Disabling or losing the permission stops new issuance. Console-only
`civs settings <city UUID> <setting> <on|off>` supports bounded operational setup;
the service still checks the owner's testing permission when enabling a setting.

ArcCore host 0.1.19 and bundled Core helpers 2.7.55 are mandatory; the shared
API is byte-identical to the host API. ArcCore provides lifecycle/scheduling,
SQL, atomic files, localized text, native dialogs/tables, packet displays and the host-owned asynchronous
schematic service. Human residents use Core's `PaperPacketNpcs` and
`PacketPlayerNpc` with bundled signed skins, equipment and work-only arm swings.
No resident Bukkit entity, native inventory or hitbox is registered. Core owns
profile/spawn/metadata packets, viewer/chunk lifecycle and validated interactions;
ArcCivs owns authoritative positions, paths, work and health. Resident nameplates
and courier parcels attach to the same packet actor through an admitted per-viewer
carrier lease. Skins are stable per worker UUID; no profile network lookup occurs.
Work props never enter city storage. Supplied personal tools, armor, food and consumables are recorded separately in the city snapshot. Residents expose a native
inventory menu backed by 33 saved slots: six equipment slots and 27 bag slots. Quick transfers preserve
item metadata and use the same inventory journal; returned items go to the player's
inventory, not the warehouse. One bread heals six health points. The legacy supply
action offers up to 16 bread; the native bag uses its actual slot capacity. Armor
equips native slots; a supplied matching iron work tool reduces incoming
damage by ten percent. Props alone confer no bonus. Residents start with 20 health;
each new completed profession cycle earns one internal point, with ten points per
level and a level-five cap. The highest profession level adds two maximum health
per extra level, up to 28. New cycles train the currently assigned profession;
skill progress is persisted separately by profession and remains after reassignment.
Old cycles grant no retroactive points. Production
recipes consume actual inputs; gathering requires the corresponding world work.
The worker tool request is derived from the current active assignment and equipment,
not a persisted quest or reward. It transfers one real matching tool: lowest material
tier first, then the existing enchantment/durability ordering. The debit uses the
existing native inventory journal. Consumable requests target one native stack,
minus personal and local stocks; ingredients missing for the next recipe take
priority over reserve top-ups. The same red/green request action appears in the
resident card and building menu. Personal donations accept partial amounts and
consume carried plain ingredients before the warehouse remainder in the existing
work receipt. Renamed/custom supplies are excluded from automatic consumption.

Damage from players, monsters and the environment enters the virtual resident
damage owner. The actor stops work while its injury waits for the current city
write; health or permanent removal is committed before visual health/despawn changes.
Failed/unknown injury commits remain frozen until a successful canonical reload.
There is no native regeneration. Viewer/chunk retirement preserves stored health
and personal inventory; it cannot heal or resurrect a dead worker. Nearby idle
monsters approach residents through bounded round-robin checks; existing monster
targets take priority. `CivsResidentDamageEvent` is the cancellable integration
boundary for virtual residents; native `EntityDamageEvent` listeners do not receive
fake entity events. Player attacks also enforce world PvP and optional Lands
attack permission. Death destroys carried
supplies without drops or XP and frees the job/population slot. Free arrivals
refill vacancies below five residents, including before hall completion. Above
five, opposite-sex adults form families in completed houses: 20 active city
minutes per birth, then 40 to adulthood. Children use half-scale player NPCs,
play and wander, and cannot take jobs. Progress never catches up offline.
Working adults lose one hunger per active minute and consume existing bread
from their bag; hunger at or below six doubles work time. Family, hunger and
member-name changes use the existing city write queue, without starving behind work.
`CivsResidentNavigation` separates terrain capture, an asynchronous A* job and
the packet movement controller. Up to 48 pending routes share one Core bounded
worker. The worker consumes detached numeric collision shapes and captured
permission/cost decisions; it never calls Bukkit or route-policy callbacks.
Small demanded neighbourhoods are captured on the owner thread through the
service's shared `BudgetedTaskQueue` (512 operations or four milliseconds per
tick across native work). A standalone navigation owner uses 384 operations or
one millisecond. These are aggregate budgets between indivisible native calls,
not a promise that copying collision geometry is free. Each route admits at
most 4096 search nodes and 32768 captured cells. Missing chunks are unavailable;
search never loads them, and an unknown frontier cannot prove that no path exists.
A per-job heuristic weight preserves ordinary cost ordering for trips up to
32 blocks, then rises linearly to two at 64 blocks. This retains local road
preferences and follows MineColonies' bounded search approach for longer trips;
minimum-cost routes are not guaranteed there. Route-local terrain is released on completion or
cancellation, and replanning captures fresh data. Cancellation and lifecycle
tokens fence late results; shutdown observes the owned worker's drain.
Edges retain a 0.6-by-1.8-block body, fractional support and the existing step
height limit. Ladder entry, climb and exit are explicit actions: the controller
centres the actor before climbing. Door opening remains owner-thread movement.
Movement revalidates live collision and support before accepting a step and
spends a tick's remaining distance across clear waypoints, without
banking time behind an obstruction or cutting through a step or corner.
On descending terrain, horizontal walking speed and bounded falling velocity
advance independently. Swept feet and the actual packet chord still validate
collision; reaching a lower intermediate cell does not insert a landing pause.
An interrupted ladder route can settle onto real support in its current column.
Preparation and replanning never consume the walking timeout or stall clock.
Capacity, capture and search-budget failures back off and retry without safe
teleport recovery. Work checks the actual position independently of planner
completion. A physically failed or stuck route may recover to a validated loaded
safe endpoint, resetting footsteps.
The same arrival, work and cycle-token checks still apply. Actual work-route
crossings use Trails 2.3.20's `TrailsFootTrafficActor` entry point; Trails retains
the ordinary permission, protection, terrain progression and persistence pipeline.
Completed work trips teach a bounded shared corridor preference (32 cities,
512 outdoor cells each, 16 admitted cells per tick); authored buildings and
fields remain excluded. Terrain conversion belongs solely to Trails. Support
height includes paths and slabs. Player movement is never cancelled or rewritten
by resident contact. Overlapping residents yield by at most 0.05 blocks per tick
into supported, collision-free space or a free side. Contact never writes player
velocity; even a trapped resident remains passable. Navigation still yields to
real players without creating native NPC entities. Native world work uses a
three-block eye-to-focus reach and unobstructed ray, stopping travel as soon as
that real block is reachable. Storage accepts any visible valid side of its
actual barrel. A courier shows one compact back parcel only while carrying goods;
it remains inside the normal resident's horizontal body envelope.
No duplicated pools, schematic cache or display transport. No schematic IO or future waiting on the gameplay thread.

Construction uses the host's paced schematic overload: at most one accepted
block or indivisible pair every five ticks. Its primary-thread readiness check
requires an arrived, stationary, awake builder for the current order. Unavailable
builders suspend native placement without discarding durable material credit.
Accepted native writes drive the builder's held material, gaze, swing and local
feedback; cosmetic failures cannot invalidate an already accepted write.
The builder remains at the worksite through native completion and durable commit.
Committed batches enqueue neighbour physics for their actual changed cells;
Paper receives both neighbour and client-update flags so unsupported plants
disappear on clients as well as on the server.

## Network authority

Every backend has an explicit, stable typed `server-id` and the same shared SQL
configuration. Core's `PaperSqlServices` owns the provider lease; blocking runtime
creation, migration and JDBC work run on workers. Shutdown seals admissions,
drains admitted file-to-SQL chains and SQL work, then releases the lease. There
is no local write fallback when SQL fails and no supported mixed old/new writer
deployment. Missing identity/SQL configuration stops startup with an actionable
error; settings changes and initial SQL failure recovery require a restart.

`arc_civs_city` stores the canonical validated settlement snapshot, immutable
home server/owner, revision and update time. `arc_civs_member.player_id` is globally
unique for both owners and members. A short registry row lock serializes writes
and multi-city imports; each save still enforces home and revision CAS. The only
successful same-revision retry is the exact committed state. Membership conflict
rolls the transaction back before any publication, world changes or start grant.
An unknown commit result suspends the affected authority until canonical reload.
`CivsStore` owns the one defensive snapshot copy before async admission;
`CivsNetworkStore` persists that same immutable revision without making a second copy.

Existing city JSON imports atomically, preserving originals. An existing SQL
city with the same home/owner wins over its stale JSON; conflicting identity or
membership fails closed. SQL city writes never update the legacy files. Immutable
construction plans and captured schematics remain local to the home backend.
`maximum-settlements` is per home backend, as the old file limit was per runtime.

Only `loadOwned(server-id)` records reach protection, construction and resident actors.
Foreign snapshots occupy a separate UI-only map and cannot be mutated or paid
out locally. `/civs` reads membership afresh on every open; Refresh repeats it,
with no background sync. Read failure shows an inline error and never presents
founding from unknown membership. The remote overview offers Refresh and a
typed Core Bungee backend request. SENT means requested, not arrival or teleport
to the city. Empty allowed-worlds makes a backend view-only.

## Domain

Eleven building types: TOWN_HALL, BUILDER_HUT, WAREHOUSE, HOUSE, FORESTER,
QUARRY, FARM, SAWMILL, BLACKSMITH, STONEMASON, LIBRARY; levels1..3.
The curated catalog contains99 assets across33
style/type families. Each schematic has one `minecraft:structure_void` primary-hut
marker inside the building; it is the physical control position, not the authored
anchor or visual entrance. A selected block's X/Y/Z is the schematic anchor without
an added height offset. The authored marker is transformed independently from that
anchor and rotation. Clicking a BARREL uses the same selected-block contract as any
other material; it does not align the internal marker to the clicked barrel.
Control-position lookup reads lightweight schematic marker metadata rather than
preparing full placement geometry.
The visible entrance is independent. Confirming a SITE grants one BARREL at that authored marker for free,
without pasting the completed building or charging a barrel as construction material.
The same city commit records its expected prior block in `pendingControls` and protects
the marker before native installation. Recovery accepts that prior block or an existing
BARREL, preserves its inventory, then retires the receipt. The same durable receipt can
prepare up to three entrance cells once: at most one free DIRT support and two AIR
cells. Ordinary terrain and crafted blocks, including lava, may be replaced; bedrock,
TileState/container contents and external protection remain guarded. This preparation
is not a periodic repair. Completed grants never regenerate
on a timer; normal block protection prevents harvesting controls, while external deletion
requires restoring the missing barrel. Members may restore an eligible missing
control barrel on an ACTIVE building; existing control blocks remain protected.
A conflicting block is retained and reported.
The first pioneer arrives after 30s,
then one per 60s while a viewer is nearby; each arrival commits its remaining
receipt before spawning. A restart never grants extra residents or catches up multiple arrivals. At night, residents shelter in their active house when
available and otherwise use the town hall, then enter the native sleeping pose.
After a physically failed night route or its full distance-based walking
timeout, the existing shared safe-recovery budget can move them into a freshly
validated, loaded home position. Preparation time does not spend that timeout;
planner capacity and search/capture budgets cannot trigger this recovery. A blocked
or occupied destination retains the failed attempt for a later pulse. Sleep
arrival grants no work credit, delivery or synthetic footsteps. The pose chooses
a clear direction using the full sleeping body, skin, armour and idle arm motion;
it lifts only the rendered pose above the floor and restores clear original feet
on waking. Support and standing headroom are rechecked during sleep. If the head
is blocked, the shared recovery budget can select a clear spot in the same home
through a clear swept sleeping envelope; without room the low pose is retained
without work or travel. A cramped shelter does not start a sleeping pose.
Idle residents choose safe loaded wandering targets within eight blocks of their
workplace, otherwise home or town hall, at 0.55 of base motion speed. Base motion is
0.215 blocks per tick; productive journeys use 0.8 of that, or 3.44 blocks/second
at 20 TPS before the current Trails surface multiplier. Idle and combat movement
retain their existing factors. Routes avoid horizontal footprints
of foreign buildings and farm fields; idle wandering does not generate Trails wear.
Productive journeys continue reporting real footsteps to Trails. Turning reduces
accumulated yaw before choosing the shortest bounded turn, including route corners.
The bounded search between world-work cycles holds the current position instead
of starting an idle trip; food, threats and night shelter still take priority.
Idle/evening campfire visits use existing lit campfires within 24 blocks of the
resident's anchor. Discovery examines at most one loaded chunk per pulse, caches
at most 256 chunks for 120 seconds and invalidates affected chunks on block events.
Six distinct safe standing positions avoid controls, fire and occupied seats;
travel expires after 45 seconds and rest lasts 25–45 seconds. Work, cargo and
night sheltering supersede leisure. No chunks are loaded for discovery.
All route segments, safe endpoints and collision nudges reject lit campfire
cells touching the resident's body or support. Execution rechecks live heat;
a resident already touching fire may leave it. Fire damage remains active.
Optional resource-pack speech uses a stable voice profile per worker UUID.
Only human viewers within 12 blocks hear ambient speech; sleeping or moving
residents do not speak ambient lines. Per-resident intervals are 15–25 seconds,
with a three-second shared minimum. Every intentional click greets its viewer,
independently of the ambient cooldown. Campfire, sleep and actual speech diagnostics are emitted on transitions.
The shared sound pack exposes all four existing clips of each voice profile to
each speech event. Client selection remains random and can repeat a clip; ambient
volume is 0.65 and a deliberate interaction reply remains 0.85. Diagnostics
distinguish the voice event and ambient versus interaction trigger.
One eligible nearby city per pulse fills vacant jobs in one persisted revision,
builders first, one warehouse courier next, producers next, town-hall scribes last. Existing assignments and completed-cycle tokens
are preserved; full jobs and inactive production sites are skipped.

The SITE hall supplies the initial four beds and a depot. A SITE builder hut
has one assignable slot: its builder can construct that same hut first, then
other buildings. Completed builder huts and ordinary town halls have one/two/three
worker slots at levels one/two/three, as existing production buildings already do.
Ordinary hall workers are scribes: one observed 60-second work session at the
workplace converts three plain PAPER and one LEATHER into one BOOK. Inputs,
output, cycle token and internal profession point commit in the same receipt.
SAWMILL accepts finite orders from unpaid active construction and operating
supply demand, followed by a small style-specific warehouse stock target.
One staffed active warehouse per city holds at most97 target items across11
kinds; level-two container targets retain their recipe unlock. Existing
warehouse/workshop/bag/cargo stock is counted once. Real demand and its raw
ingredients take priority; an optional batch needs its actual next inputs and
both workshop and destination space. No buffer work issues free materials.
The existing city-wide async supply snapshot allocates one
native-sized recipe batch per carpenter, including real intermediate batches.
Level one unlocks structural woodwork, level two containers and equipment,
and level three advanced workstations. Generic native ingredient tags accept
mixed supported wood species. Each accepted batch has an independent job ID,
frozen exact inputs/output and recipe version; actor pickup and completion
callbacks revalidate that identity after city waits. Courier cargo, carried
ingredients, other accepted batches and exact output slots are reserved once.
Workshop-bound durable ingredients remain available to planning while physical
readiness still requires actual receipt into the barrel and resident bag.
The job intent is transient; existing durable inventory receipts retain exact
resident/cycle/tool changes and native output slots for recovery. Stripping
consumes the carried log and real axe durability in that same receipt. An
upgrading workshop pauses its own production. BLACKSMITH crafts one plain
iron tool with its ordinary ingot/stick recipe; STONEMASON converts four STONE
into four STONE_BRICKS; LIBRARY converts BOOK, FEATHER and INK_SAC into one
WRITABLE_BOOK. All use 60-second observed work, fixed per-cycle
recipes and one/two/three slots. The same receipt consumes inputs, credits output
and commits the cycle and profession point for all processors.
Missing inputs or a full warehouse
retain the completed trip and retry after five seconds, without granting output
or points. Houses have no work slots. Active item-storage warehouses have one/two/three
courier slots; autoassignment fills one courier before producers and leaves extra
warehouse slots for explicit assignment. Legacy stock-only cities
retain hall builders; so do migrated full cities without room for a hut.
Population remains4/8/12 by hall level,12maximum; houses add2/4/6beds. Building
limit16 and native NPC limit48 remain. Completed hall upgrades expand the actual
persisted radius by32, including96→128→160 for new default cities. New founding
reserves future maximum spacing; existing cities keep their actual radius.

Materials pages show8 rows and provide one explicit player supply action plus
an admin/Creative deficit grant. Both subtract existing builder stock and fit physical
slot capacity. Player supply journals exact plain-slot before/after debit together
with container credits, locks the source inventory, and uses Core native player
data persistence before applying container credits. Recovery waits for an offline
source player; unknown slot drift remains a conflict. Initial/reload item changes
wait for local receipt discovery; exact locks are installed and unrelated players
are released before network city loading begins. Local journal failure remains
explicitly blocked instead of being treated as an empty journal. Old receipts
without player debits remain compatible. No full player state is restored or virtual surplus created.

World capture/revalidation shares up to2048 cells across four scans per tick,
yielding after the2ms budget between native calls and rotating the next scan.
A single native call is not preempted. Builder availability is checked before
schematic recovery/capture and again after asynchronous work. Scan completion
logs phase, cell count and elapsed time independently of SQL timings.

Items replace the closed WOOD/STONE/FOOD ledger. One-time founder reserve is
96OAK_LOG,96COBBLESTONE,24BREAD. Legacy remaining stock converts1:1 exactly once
when its old paid construction is complete; legacy snapshots and orders decode
unchanged. Virtual maps contain plain obtainable vanilla materials only; custom
items remain untouched in physical inventory. There is no Vault or token payout.

PHYSICAL is the default and uses only actual native items. Each building's real
27-slot control barrel is its primary warehouse. All CHEST/TRAPPED_CHEST/BARREL
blocks inside its 3D bounds are indexed automatically. A double chest contributes
each eligible 27-slot half once. Existing explicit city registrations remain
compatible. Discovery reads loaded chunk tile entities on geometry registration
and chunk load, updates individual placement/break events, and refreshes affected
building chunks after a paste. Availability and production never rescan world
blocks or chunk tile entities. Unloaded chunks are not loaded for storage.

VIRTUAL is an explicit opt-in with paginated warehouses of1728/3456/5184items by
building level. The control barrel remains the visible input/output for transfers.
An owner or city member with management access may explicitly rebind only a legacy
ACTIVE building's control through its building menu. The authored marker barrel is
installed automatically through the same one-time receipt; upgrade confirmation also
performs this rebind when needed. The old barrel and its inventory are never moved
or cleared; the old container is
automatically registered as an additional warehouse within the 16-container limit.
The city UUID and stocks remain unchanged. SITE buildings and buildings with an
accepted construction cannot be rebound.
PHYSICAL consumes loaded containers only; VIRTUAL consumes its durable maps.
Existing common/building reserves in PHYSICAL are pending delivery, not invisible
spendable materials. Materialization uses the same durable slot receipt, examines
at most 128 containers and changes at most 1024 slots per attempt, preserving any
remainder when full. Control barrels have priority; other containers rotate fairly
even after a full/no-op attempt. Each selected container is read at most once. Opening
a physical warehouse or switching to PHYSICAL attempts it; nearby cities with
pending items also retry one city per5s fairly. Mode changes create no items.
Deposit in VIRTUAL moves actual control-barrel cargo to that building's
warehouse; withdrawal moves up to64 items to its barrel, using its own warehouse
then the common initial/legacy reserve. No direct player-inventory automation.

Scribes and the four processors remain observed 60-second work sessions with
real ingredients and outputs. FARM, FORESTER and QUARRY are physical worksites,
not timed item grants: FARM tills real soil, plants and waits for mature crops
before harvesting native drops (1.5 seconds per action). No new bread-baking jobs
are created; a legacy BREAD selection resolves to wheat harvesting.
When a complete bounded field pass finds no harvest or field preparation job, FARM tends an
unripe crop for four seconds. Watering maintains soil moisture and advances only
age 3 to 4; optional fertilizer consumes one real bone meal at age 5 to reach 6.
Care requires an actual soil or growth change. Each farmer indexes the whole field in
16-cell slices, including while walking or working, and retains known targets across
receipts. Harvest outranks care, selects a nearby known ripe crop, and reserves its
target against other farmers; new maturity can replace unfinished care without
reusing its work timer. Each selected target is rechecked live before the existing
receipt validation. Exposed rain suppresses manual watering, including its free age checkpoint; covered plots may still need care. Native age checkpoints
bound each growth-cycle bonus without a separate timer or restart catch-up.
Temporary city saves pause work admission without deleting residents' scan cursors,
partial field passes or valid targets. An incomplete field search holds the farmer
in place; waiting for growth requires a completed pass without actionable work.
Care retains field reach, protection and receipt validation; it grants no drops,
profession training or hoe wear. Existing hunger still applies while working.
FORESTER fells a real tree of at most 128 logs per
action, taking max(6 seconds, 1 second per log), yields native tool drops and
replants only after spending locally stocked saplings: one for ordinary trees,
four in a 2×2 plot for dark oak. Each tree receipt records at most 128 exact
physical block changes. Native Unbreaking durability applies only to changed
non-air source blocks.
QUARRY advances an authored real shaft across multiple floors. Before excavation,
the miner needs a pickaxe and carried ladders, torches and cobblestone. If any
supply runs out, one storage receipt tops the bag up to 16 ladders, 8 torches and
32 cobblestone before work resumes; it does not alter blocks or mining progress.
Mine plan V2 first completes the 3×3 shaft around its continuous ladder and backing,
preserving the paid landing platforms. Its outer shell is secured before widening;
the old inner walls and gallery ceilings never refill the shaft. One rung ahead
provides a visible working stance, but each landing's seven platforms and first
light are completed before extending the ladder below that floor. It then finishes
each floor's main corridor, left arm and right arm in that order. Fixed torch
positions survive continuation and upgrades. Each mine's nullable `minePlans`
entry stores version 2, its next operation and the legacy cutover ordinal. A legacy
`mineProgress` value remains unchanged for audit: V2 inspects the real world from
its own start, skips satisfied cells and commits the new cursor only with the first
physical item/world receipt. Unknown versions and out-of-plan cursors fail closed.
Deletion removes both maps; an upgrade resets both cursors and reuses satisfied
native work. This is forward migration: a pre-V2 runtime does not understand the
widened geometry and must not resume quarry work against it after a rollback.
Mining secures exposed floor, walls, ceiling and lighting with paid carried supplies
before advancing, and harvests native drops one block per 2-second action. The three
worksites therefore do not mint resources on a timer. The miner can reach visible
blocks up to 4.5 blocks away; collision and line of sight still control each action.
Mining work positions stay inside the authored shaft, transfer and gallery interiors;
a nearby natural cave outside that volume is never a valid stance. Cached work and
actual work arrival enforce the same boundary. The underground work route also
stays in those passages; the outdoor approach to the
upper ladder keeps ordinary city routing. After a failed final approach, the
worksite excludes that stance briefly and selects another supported, visible position
for the same action. Temporary planner congestion, unloaded chunks and player
collisions do not reject a stance; work begins only after real arrival.
If falling blocks refill the authored clearance above the current shaft floor,
the miner physically clears that obstruction before securing the floor. These
recovery actions retain the current shaft step and use the same native drops,
protection, work duration and durable receipt as ordinary excavation.
Item-storage processors physically collect ingredients from their own control barrel
before working. Both work admission and the final receipt require carried ingredients;
output remains in the building store and couriers move it to its current endpoint.
FARM and FORESTER perform work directly at field/tree cells, without a per-cell
return trip. Legacy abstract-stock producers retain their old return trip. The chosen processor output is persisted
per building; a full destination pauses delivery. Farming takes 1.5 seconds per
physical action (3 seconds when hungry), retaining native crop growth and drops.
Without the owner's effective `arccivs.chunkload` permission, residents retire
outside viewer range. With fresh owner permission and a complete set of native
ENTITY_TICKING plugin tickets, the same physical simulation runs without viewers;
there is no elapsed-time catch-up or synthetic crop growth. LuckPerms lookup uses
static/server plus settlement-world context, supports offline owners, defaults
to deny, refreshes every 15 seconds and expires positive answers after 30 seconds.
Chunk admission covers the settlement square plus one chunk margin, generates no
new terrain, admits whole cities within a global configurable 2048-chunk budget,
limits pending loads to four and acquisitions to two per tick, and releases leases
on permission loss, city removal, reload, world unload and shutdown. Core owns
reference-counted native ticket leases. Nearby viewers retain NPC admission priority.

## Warehouse logistics

Orders are derived from local ingredient shortages: one recipe cycle per assigned
worker, less local stock and incoming cargo. Construction orders cover only the
unpaid material remainder, delivered to a builder hut. Builders collect useful batches
from their own huts into real inventory slots; placement consumes only carried items.
A full builder bag can return spare ordinary blocks to its hut to fit the next row.
Starting another builder session with sufficient carried supplies goes directly
to work, including CONSTRUCTING between placement credits. A real deficit still
requires the physical hut pickup. Storage-trip diagnostics distinguish departure
reason and route planning from the committed pickup, including no-op pickups.
A SITE hut can
bootstrap its own construction; neither the target building nor the hall is a
construction buffer. Explicit virtual mode uses the same hut-local scope.
All active buildings can use their own indexed internal containers, excluding other buildings'
controls. SITE and CONSTRUCTING buildings retain control-only local storage. Exact-source
maintenance restores former MineColonies rack cells to empty chests/barrels; it preserves
ordinary decorative bookshelves, player-changed blocks, occupied shelves and PDC data.
The console-only `repair-storage-racks` command compares the exact private legacy/current
schemes without changing accepted construction captures. One city-wide
supply dispatcher captures native holders one 27-slot half/barrel per shared Core budget step.
Only detached stock, capacity, requests, excess, positions and courier tokens enter its bounded
CPU executor. A shared ledger assigns all couriers together: construction first, then waiting
ordinary requests/collection with trip-distance and courier fairness. Source units, destination
partial stacks/empty slots and producer meetings cannot be booked twice. Accepted empty trips
survive unrelated city revisions; relevant native generations and domain inputs fence new
results. Inventory events request refreshes; a fresh capture every 5 seconds also detects writers
which bypass events. Stale inputs coalesce into a new calculation; failed trips receive backoff.
Reload/close fence and drain jobs. Physical pickup freshly checks source surplus, demand, bag
room, destination room and actual arrival before the existing durable receipt. Temporary
reservations end only before pickup or at a definitive receipt outcome; unknown commits remain
under recovery authority. Carried cargo remains durable even if its destination becomes full.
Source-to-destination entrances must be within the existing
88-block work-route bound. Warehouses use their own indexed native containers;
ordinary producer sources and destinations are strictly their control barrels.
Worksite orders include one spare IRON_HOE, IRON_AXE or IRON_PICKAXE per assigned
worker, according to job; FARM requests only its selected seed type.

`CivWorker.cargo` records one material, at most64 items, original source entrance
and stable shipment identity. New cargo is marked `inventoryBacked`; its exact items
occupy the resident's real bag and are reserved against personal consumption/removal.
Legacy cargo materializes in a durable revision before gameplay exposure. Existing full bags
or old unstackable shipments retain overflow in extra persisted native-item slots, visible
on withdrawal-only inventory pages; ordinary pickup/deposit capacity remains 27 bag slots. A pending
old receipt retires first, followed by that migration. Pickup journals source debit,
bag items and cargo together;
the actor waits for that receipt before travelling. Unload journals destination
credit, exact bag debit, cargo removal, one completed cycle and one WAREHOUSE profession point.
An empty courier without another shipment returns beside its assigned warehouse
barrel and waits there; this return is real movement with no cargo or work reward.
Night sheltering still takes priority. Actual dirt paths and provider-recognized
roads have routing cost1, remembered unworn corridors1.5, ordinary ground2.25.
This changes route preference, not speed; Trails owns widening and block wear.
Planning revalidates demand and actual stock at pickup; incoming cargo prevents
duplicate orders, including legacy cargo whose actual destination is now a hut.
Construction destinations are chosen within the current courier's route bound,
so an unreachable staffed hut does not hide another reachable hut.
A pending receipt retains the actor's shipment plan. Shipment recovery preserves
its recorded source and accepted cargo; safe empty-return or night-shelter
recovery changes no shipment state. Missing destinations return to a reachable surviving warehouse
or hall, within the same route bound. Reassignment
does not discard carried goods. Death transfers exact equipment and bag contents to
a saved corpse for five minutes and releases its shipment reservation. The corpse
shows its resident and killer/cause; city members can loot nearby through the same
native item journal. Expiry removes unclaimed contents. Rendering creates no loot
copies, and the body preserves fractional feet height on paths, slabs and stairs.
Corpse presentation searches at most 25 loaded columns, up to 24 blocks below the
saved death height. The existing sleeping-body envelope must fit with native support
under head, torso and legs, and the standing resurrection envelope must be safe.
No safe pose hides presentation without changing the persisted death or its items.
Every action resolves the current pose and exact `diedAt`, so stale body controls
cannot target a later death of the same worker. Auto corpse access requires admin.
An admin-issued tagged paper scroll uses the existing durable inventory receipt:
persist exact native player-slot debit, apply and flush player data, save/publish
the same worker identity and remaining belongings, then retire the receipt. An
untouched debit can abort when the corpse expires or its pose changes; an applied
debit completes the accepted transition on recovery. Admission prefers the accepted
body site through normal loaded-space checks and never moves an existing actor.
Auto control barrels permit nearby ordinary inspection but all inventory mutation
paths require admin; receipt locks still override admin writes. Abstract Auto stock
remains separate from native barrel contents and `inventory` stays null.
Autonomous idle residents use the prepared building entrance as their roaming
anchor, including a SITE whose authored control is not reachable yet.
Full destinations keep cargo and retry, and ordinary open containers remain open.

Hungry residents with no personal bread fetch up to four existing bread from their
town hall, limited by its stock and real bag capacity, before returning to their job.
Monster attacks interrupt work: nearby adult residents assist, while children and
badly injured adults retreat. Combat uses actual carried weapons and native monster
damage events; neither NPC combat nor corpse rendering grants currency or player XP.

All item movement uses exact native slot before/after receipts stored by Core
AtomicFileStore on the I/O executor before native writes. Synchronous planning
freezes source membership, then only native containers changed by the exact receipt
stay locked through journal retirement. Unaffected containers remain open and
interactive. Explicit transfers close affected viewers; NPC/background transfers
use open containers immediately and keep their views open, with only receipt
containers' item edits briefly fenced until retirement. A source player's inventory remains locked
through its item receipt. It checks all
changed slots before any writes; restart may finish only known before/after states.
Drift is an explicit conflict. The city revision commits after native application;
only an exact same-revision retry is accepted. Unknown outcomes retain the receipt,
freeze city mutations and reconcile against canonical SQL on reload. Native chunk
persistence and SQL are separate systems: this is guarded replay, not a distributed
atomic transaction; a power-loss rollback of already-acknowledged chunk data remains
a platform persistence boundary. No blind repeat after an unknown result.

Construction starts without requiring the complete material list. New orders split the
immutable captured before/after plan into deterministic horizontal rows of at most16
adjacent cells with identical historical material keys. Free water does not
change saved row boundaries: water blocks, waterlogging and cauldron filling no
longer require water buckets, while the underlying block/cauldron still does.
Old unpaid water requests are recalculated; paid reservations are not refunded.
One observed builder
session adds at most 64 existing rows after two seconds of work. Concurrent
builders add once-only durable credit, bounded by remaining rows and 768 total rows;
one placer still charges and installs each row serially. Durable work credit
preserves the remaining authorization across pause/restart; it does
not change row indices or charge already paid rows again. Only that row's exact obtainable items are debited, in the existing
durable inventory receipt together with the READY→PLACING transition and `reservedBatch`.
Missing items persist WAITING_MATERIALS without any debit; one waiting city is retried
per5s in round-robin order. Open warehouse viewers do not defer it. Adding
the required items resumes automatically. `materials` holds the unpaid remainder.
Paid rows retain their reservation across pause, retry and restart, so replay cannot charge
twice. Completing the row clears its reservation and advances within the bounded
session while credit remains; the next trip is required when that credit ends. Legacy
orders with null payment state retain their already-paid 256-cell tile algorithm.
New SITE plans and accepted ACTIVE-building upgrades for the next level share a
persistent FIFO queue, with only one active construction. The unique marker cell is omitted from new paste plans;
matching existing target blocks need no replacement. Core's provider-owned paste
splits new placement around the control into at most six crops, applied sequentially
in one batch while preserving the barrel and its inventory. Durable completion
advances the plan, and callback fencing includes the order and expected cursor, not
only the UUID. Revalidation checks only the batch about to be placed: current order
identity, loaded chunks/borders, Lands authority, bedrock and nonempty containers.
It does not compare future cells to their originally captured world preimage or keep
extra per-cell world snapshots. The immutable captured schematic remains tied to the
accepted plan, but owners and members may alter its cells at every construction phase.
Committed batches are not checked or restored if a player later breaks or rebuilds
them. If a crash interrupts an uncommitted batch, that batch may be applied again;
its durable material reservation prevents a second charge, but there is no per-cell
exactly-once guarantee.

Paired and multi-block cells such as doors, beds and double blocks are applied as
one native placement unit, so a batch cannot leave only half of a pair. Finished
buildings retain native physics; only the active placement and exact item-receipt
cells are fenced while their operation is in progress, and receipt cells stay locked
through commit. After the durable build commit, neighbor physics is issued for exact
changed plan cells, including updates needed by vines beside removed logs. Cosmetic
shape repair is limited to exact-source fences, panes and walls; it skips AIR and
replacement blocks and never rebuilds a removed or remodeled structure.

World placement validates ownership, loaded chunks, borders, Lands, building boxes
and foreign controlpoints. It preserves nonempty inventories and registered storage.
Owners and members may place and break blocks inside their buildings, SITE plans and
accepted upgrades at every construction phase, with normal drops. Existing control
barrels remain protected (eligible missing ACTIVE controls may be restored), and exact
live receipt cells remain locked. Stranger, environmental and other-plugin
schematic guards remain active; owner/member editing does not bypass Lands, bedrock,
nonempty-container or construction identity checks. Native control inventory remains
accessible to members.
Personal cyan boundary cues use Core packet displays within24blocks of an edge,
with at most60parts per viewer and no perimeter/world scans.
The same owner and settlement pulse maintain three-line RU/EN holograms above the
saved control barrels: building name, current level and construction state or
assigned worker count. Only the nearest four controls within16blocks are shown;
labels respect terrain occlusion and never use the paste origin or a future upgrade
marker. State changes update existing packet handles. Leaving, deletion, reload and
shutdown retire them with the existing scene; no physical entities or world scan.

SITE plans may be placed while a different building is under construction. A
persistent ordered list of building IDs holds requested SITE jobs, with one active
construction. Duplicate requests are idempotent. Starting the next job reloads its
saved plan and captures/revalidates current terrain. A failed queue head retains
its position and exposes a concrete retry reason; it is not silently skipped.
Upgrades retain their existing active-order contract.

Building and city removal first persist a deletion intent in the city snapshot.
The service loads only the exact control chunks asynchronously, then revalidates
registered controls and Lands access on the main thread. Only BARREL controls are
removed; native contents are dropped with metadata, and the free barrel itself is
not granted as an item. Already absent/replaced controls are preserved on retry.
Final city deletion retains the existing tombstone contract; removing a queued or
active building clears its corresponding task and lets the next job proceed.
World blocks and dropped entities retain native save durability; this is not a
second escrow or a cross-system atomic transaction.

## Migration and recovery visibility

Ordinary in-flight city saves leave menus available. Mutating player actions wait
for the city's current write and re-read membership, online status, distance and
operation-specific state before executing; no stale snapshot is replayed. Waiting
actions resume before new NPC work on the owning server tick. Unknown writes and
actual recovery retain their hard barriers. Administrative reload still requires
all writes and placements to drain.

All loaded inventory receipts acquire native container locks before the service
publishes readiness. Recovery may process one receipt per pulse, but players
cannot alter later receipts' before/after slots while they wait. A known SQL
rejection preserves the founding preview; an unknown commit result freezes
changes until canonical reload.

A migrated city already containing sixteen buildings and no builder hut retains
one builder assignment in its active hall. This narrowly avoids an irreversible
bootstrap deadlock in old full cities. New city placement reserves a hut slot,
so the compatibility condition is unreachable through the new normal flow.

Creative profession-tool requests can issue one plain requested iron tool directly into the resident's main hand without a player stack. Existing held equipment moves into a real free bag slot. Survival still transfers the cheapest matching native player item; repeated requests recheck the equipped tool after the durable inventory receipt.

Transient schematic WORLD_UNAVAILABLE/chunk-unloaded failures preserve the current paid row and retry no sooner than five seconds later, only when its destination entrance is loaded. They do not become permanent generic placement failures. Pre-upgrade generic paused rows receive one guarded recovery attempt per load; a repeated permanent error stays paused. Native world protection and nonempty-container checks remain mandatory.

## Constructed medieval layout and Mine V5

`CivsSettlementLayout` is the detached deterministic planner used by placement and
offline preview exports. It reserves future footprints and work fields, places
residences and civic buildings in the core and producers outside, and connects
accepted entrances with graded roads and a gated perimeter. The first infrastructure
receipt pins the largest safe supported ring; existing accepted sites are never
moved. A partial legacy perimeter reports exact blocking building IDs and coordinates.
ROAD/GATE preserve explicit transit columns; native collision and headroom remain
authoritative. There are at most 96 infrastructure pieces in addition to 16 ordinary
sites. Reaching this infrastructure limit cannot starve ordinary upgrades.

Infrastructure is encoded as bounded entity-free Sponge v3, decoded by the existing
Core schematic provider, and built through the ordinary immutable plan and payment
receipts. It has no control barrel, housing, storage or work slots. Player construction
menus do not expose these internal pieces. A stair's neighbour-derived corner shape
may change during construction; autonomous revalidation ignores only that property,
while preserving material, orientation, half, waterlogged and protection checks.

Mine V5 adds paid timber frames, lower-level perimeter walls, rails around the central
opening and connected galleries to the existing single ladder shaft. V2/V3/V4 plans
and accepted receipt indices remain immutable. Native work uses the committed prefix
for standing admission rather than the finished mine mask, so future railings do not
reject a currently safe construction stance. Timber is collected with other supplies
when available; its absence stops only a step that actually consumes it.
