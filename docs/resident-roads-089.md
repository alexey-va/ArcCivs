# Resident cadence, plans and storage alignment — 0.1.89

Unassigned adults hand over short leisure legs on the existing five-tick worksite pulse, with at most four idle actors in addition to the eight farm actors. The shared eight-target-check budget resets only on the ordinary city pulse. Successful unassigned walks have no artificial rest beyond the five-tick handoff; failed routes keep a bounded 2.5–4s backoff. Assigned producers retain their existing 2–6.5s successful rest, including paused farms on the fast field lane, with no new work credit. Version 0.1.90 supersedes the stationary courier rendezvous: a producer may keep moving while the courier updates an ephemeral interception target. Threats, food, sleep, meetings and accepted cargo take priority. Only a ready, unblocked moving leisure route displays the walking status; other unassigned residents show a neutral status. No work credit or road wear is issued for leisure.

The autonomous profile includes one plan board after the first house, reusing its existing price and terrain/placement pipeline. Existing boards and accepted orders take precedence, and a newly optional board cannot block upgrades in an old full village. Shared footprint calculation and autonomous blueprint preparation use each building's real maximum level, including the one-level board.

Settlement menus show the next accepted queue entry or autonomous goal. During construction the read-only calculation projects only completion of that accepted building; it does not grant resources, estimate future earnings or reserve a position. Current shortages are labelled as current stock requirements. Personal towns show only accepted orders. The panel distinguishes the current build, next queued build and target after the current build; repeated current entries are omitted.

Nine private warehouse templates now use reciprocal double-chest states facing the authored shared aisle, retaining odd single chests. The generator pins its private inputs; asset bytes, coordinates and renders are not published to Git/Maven. The existing explicit storage-rack repair accepts the original shelf and old single-chest state, tests both target halves together, and skips a whole pair if either half is filled, viewed, marked, protected, externally linked, lid-blocked or otherwise changed. It does not repair worlds automatically. Accepted construction plans retain their captured bytes.

[Road maintenance](road-upkeep.md) describes bounded corridor repairs and the path/wood palette. [Resource assessment](resident-roads-089-assessment.md) records the changed floor's conditional resale and unchanged storage, job and stock rules. Automated tests and source-derived renders do not establish runtime activation or native-client appearance. The owner's current delivery instruction explicitly defers restart.
