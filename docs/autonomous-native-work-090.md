# Autonomous native work and moving couriers — 0.1.90

Known authored Auto farms and quarries now use the existing physical worksite planner. Farmers till, sow, care for and harvest the real authored field. Miners excavate and secure the existing versioned multi-level mine plan. Unknown custom schematics retain their existing abstract work behavior. Personal settlements retain their material, tool and native-output rules.

Native Auto scenery is part of the already-paid worksite. Each subaction journals exact before/after world cells, with no carried inputs, item drops, stock payout, skill award or economic worker-token advance. The typed receipt binds city, world, owner, building, worker, token and mine cursor. Recovery rechecks real chunks, protection, occupancy and world state without requiring a transient actor/planner cache. Missing authority fails closed; unavailable chunks or occupied solid placements retry. Forward natural crop growth is accepted after a sealed write, while an exact untouched preimage takes priority. Mine cursor provenance and historical plan versions remain intact.

The economic session stays separate. Only bounded observations in WORKING accumulate time; travel, path search, pending persistence, sleep, pauses and offline gaps do not. At least the existing work-duration requirement must be observed before the normal real return to the depot and unique resource receipt. Restart or actor retirement loses unfinished credit. Repeated native subactions do not duplicate economic delivery; a newly selected identical subaction can restart after the previous native receipt closes.

Auto native crop/soil and mine cells use the existing read-only protection for normal players, with the established administrator bypass. Natural authored crop growth remains allowed except during exact pending world receipts or unavailable-state protection. This protection covers the precise authored domain and the existing mine safety shell, not the empty space between mine branches. It prevents ordinary players extracting the newly supplied native scenery as a repeatable resource source. Administrative removal is not ordinary production.

Couriers intercept moving source residents. An ephemeral, bounded lead target is refreshed at most once per second after meaningful source displacement; the accepted cargo keeps its logical source and assignment. The courier does not freeze the producer. Actual pickup still requires current source identity and assignment, available surplus, loaded geometry, nearby real positions and clear line of sight. A soft allocation may shrink to currently available surplus before the durable transfer is sealed. The sealed receipt remains immutable and replayable.

Road turns now use a flat intermediate block between straight wooden stair flights. A separately paid bounded route override can correct one or two local heights without replacing historical captures or moving the route in X/Z. Offline captured-terrain replay proves half-block contacts in both directions for the reported turn; it does not prove every live route. The upper flight beside the protected quarry footprint remains one block wide.

The release also retains the 0.1.89 idle cadence, next-plan menu, optional automatic plan board and private empty-chest alignment changes. The [resource assessment](autonomous-native-090-assessment.md) records existing income rates and the precise scenery protection. Source tests, rendered geometry, immutable CI artifact, delivery, runtime activation and native-client behavior are separate evidence. The current instruction permits delivery to all three servers without restart or reload.
