# ArcCivs 0.1.89 resource assessment

The [input](resident-roads-089-assessment.json) and [calculator result](resident-roads-089-assessment-result.json) separate abstract WOOD/STONE, world floors, conditional Vault redemption, tokens and XP. Scenarios are assumptions, not measured income: 1/4/6 active cities, 0.25/1/4 active hours/day and 16/64/256 worked road columns/day.

Road maintenance keeps its existing price of one abstract STONE per actually worked column, three-second work, finite stock, next-building reserve and exact durable receipt. Width expands eligible work, not the action rate. Flats become DIRT_PATH; dry bottom stairs use the style's existing wood (oak, spruce or dark oak). A column can replace one floor and clear at most four natural headroom cells, without drops. Clearance-only work creates no floor. Existing accepted construction/maintenance keeps its original blocks and costs.

Public approaches can still be mined. The alternative adversarial case mines and sells every repaired flat floor's one DIRT drop: fresh Spawn disk quotes at 21:12:07 UTC on 25 October are 1 Vault/DIRT versus 0.05 Vault/COBBLESTONE. Wooden stairs are 0.88 Vault each. These are disk prices, not in-memory quotes or actual sales. All-flat conditional resale therefore rises from 0.80/3.20/12.80 to 16/64/256 Vault per city-day, and from 5.60/89.60/537.60 to 112/1,792/10,752 per network-week. It cannot be added to the same retained road floor. Removed cut material has no extra drop.

The loose builder ceiling remains 1,200 columns/hour before travel, sleep, cooldown and stock. Conditional all-flat resale consequently has a 1,200 nominal Vault/hour ceiling while surplus stock lasts, versus 60 before. Existing L1/L2/L3 quarry stone replenishment bounds are 180/360/540 STONE per quarry-worker-hour before travel; conditional floor resale bounds are now 180/360/540 Vault/hour, versus 9/18/27. Ore replaces stone cycles; administrative grants are not ordinary sustainable earnings. Actual sale access, incidence and throughput remain unknown. Current SELL stays current; SELL-off/contracts stays a future scenario until actually activated.

A newly automatic PLAN_BOARD uses its existing one-off price of 12 WOOD + 8 STONE after the first house. Accepted/active boards are not duplicated. Established full sixteen-plot villages can continue upgrades without fitting this new optional goal. No new bed, job, rate or cap is introduced. This is a founding-day sink, not a daily fee.

Warehouse alignment retains all 275 chest blocks and 7,425 slots across nine templates. Explicit paired repair skips filled, viewed, PDC-marked, externally linked, protected or changed containers and never moves contents. Idle handoffs award no work cycle, training, resource or Trails wear. The maximum existing path multiplier is 1.3: all-travel ratios versus old cobble are 1.015625 or 1.04; actual whole-cycle gain depends on travel fraction. Wooden stairs receive only their existing Trails behavior, with no new multiplier or stack.

Decision: accept the owner's material and corridor changes with the existing price, finite stock sink, reserve, receipt and work rate. Do not silently add protection, caps or shop repricing. Direct Vault, tokens, player XP and resident XP remain separately zero. Conditional floor interception and the one-off board sink are explicit above. Source tests, delivery, activation and observed income are separate evidence; this release is delivered without restart and has no new live acceptance claim.
