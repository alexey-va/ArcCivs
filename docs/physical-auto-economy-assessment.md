# Physical Auto supply assessment

Auto now uses ordinary native construction, production, resident food and research. Its additional privilege is explicit issuance of missing actual items to a known local demand through the existing exact slot journal. Ordinary towns remain player supplied.

Direct source-level deltas are0Vault,0tokens and0playerXP. Native item issuance is nonzero: the absolute shared backend controller ceiling is512items per accepted receipt at most1time/s, or512items/s. The low/base/high0.25/1/4hour sensitivities therefore bound gross issuance at460,800/1,843,200/7,372,800items per backend-day. These intentionally loose bounds include no idle/full/unloaded/denied/busy time and are not measured or expected production. They are shared across1/10/500cities, not multiplied by that count.

Each normal request is much smaller: one captured construction row, one current recipe/workplace buffer, an accepted research bill or4bread for an existing hungry home. The controller requires a fresh bounded supply capture and subtracts local items, carried supplies, accepted incoming cargo and exportable goods reachable through an assigned warehouse courier. Both courier legs must fit the shared route bound; an ongoing courier trip does not turn its existing stock into a new deficit. Before a courier is assigned, the helper can bootstrap a needed local input instead of waiting for transport that does not exist. A held tool also satisfies its tool-kind requirement. One city and one bounded request per pulse, round-robin request selection and the existing128-container row-search ceiling plus busy/pending-receipt fencing prevent one full request or unknown acknowledgement from creating another issuance loop.

New Auto buildings consume the same full native material bills as ordinary buildings; the former fixed W/S/F construction prices no longer fund new work. No generic price, earning multiplier or monetary rate is introduced. New research consumes the ordinary once-per-project bill. Saved already-paid construction and funded research remain paid. Native forestry yields replace the second abstract four-WOOD credit. Native road upkeep burns one actual COBBLESTONE from the builder bag or its own hut; the historical closed-mode receipt still burns one abstract STONE. The road patch and payment use one existing journal, including replay and late Lands revalidation. Exact native leaf/crop/mine outputs and native tool/input checks belong to the root-owned production patch and must be reviewed together before release.

Migration creates native items once from remaining closed reserve units. It preserves real resident/corpse bags, bread and equipment and waits for accepted sealed receipts. Unsealed paid legacy forestry jobs retain their geometry, cursor, harvested-log prefix and deferred replacement-kit flag; after migration only remaining blocks yield native outputs, without re-cutting the prefix or adding abstract WOOD. A retained deferred kit may replant0to4saplings once as a grandfathered compatibility issuance; new physical jobs never defer seed payment. WOOD uses the sole current forester species or OAK_LOG; STONE becomes COBBLESTONE; FOOD becomes BREAD; other six closed industry products map to their names; one IRON_TOOL_KIT becomes one IRON_PICKAXE. The existing ordinary pickaxe recipe is3IRON_INGOT+2STICK. Producer pending reserves retain their workplace identity and materialize only in that workplace's own containers; full/unloaded local storage keeps the remainder without depot overflow. Accepted cargo reserves retain their resolved destination. Migration is a one-time stock change, not a repeated daily flow; its actual quantity is the source snapshot and is not invented in the calculator.

New planning and automatic top-ups wait while migration reserves remain. Existing PHYSICAL work, consumption and courier movement continue against actual native slots so full local buffers can drain; only null legacy production is blocked. Full/unloaded storage preserves the unmaterialized migration remainder without making it spendable; ordinary PHYSICAL stock reads actual slots. Exact receipts support replay/partial application without a second credit. Administrator-only native inventory writes, container break/drop extraction and corpse transfer boundaries must remain effective because administrative extraction could turn these granted native items into a privileged input to current SELL/contracts. No ordinary-player withdrawal path is authorized. Current SELL/AutoSell and the planned SELL-to-contract transition remain unchanged. No current sell quotes or conversion rates are assumed.

A parent read-only seven-day network ledger check returned schema 2, 2,708 records and 23 players with separate Vault and tokens. Unknown-origin flows and concentration mean it is not used to invent an ordinary income rate or item sale conversion. The calculator evaluates explicit gross envelope scenarios and unit separation. There is no price, accumulation target or income forecast; live quantities/cadence and post-delivery behavior remain unobserved. Source tests do not establish deployment or client acceptance.
