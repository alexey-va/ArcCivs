# ArcCivs 0.1.55: one-shaft V4 network

[Calculation](mine-v4-economy-assessment.json) and
[arithmetic result](mine-v4-economy-assessment-result.json) compare the published
V3 plan with V4. These are source sensitivities, not measured income.

| Building level | Main tiers | V3 unique cut targets | V4 unique cut targets | V3 / V4 cobblestone targets | V3 / V4 ladders | V3 / V4 torches |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 3 | 2524 | 2957 | 827 / 988 | 15 / 14 | 132 / 160 |
| 2 | 4 | 3346 | 3887 | 1093 / 1290 | 19 / 18 | 176 / 210 |
| 3 | 5 | 4168 | 4817 | 1359 / 1592 | 23 / 22 | 220 / 260 |

Counts are identical across the three styles. V4 reserves the original ladder
wing, uses three primary tunnels and four side branches, and completes the
shaft and landings before branching. Target counts are not guaranteed drops
or mandatory debits: matching native blocks skip, sound backing stays, and
hazards can require additional paid repairs. Net target-count differences do
not imply that one plan's footprint contains the other. Existing mines retain
old provenance and are remodeled through ordinary physical receipts.

Real excavation retains its two-second base action and native tool-dependent
drops. The theoretical ceiling remains 1800 actions per active hour. The
post-exhaustion face retains one exclusive output per six-second base action:
95% cobblestone, 4% coal, 1% raw iron with a suitable tool. Incompatible ore
rolls become cobblestone. Its theoretical 600 items/hour, tool wear, hunger,
bag capacity, night and no-offline-progress rules are unchanged. Restoring a
blocked worker can improve actual output; no new multiplier is introduced.

The low/base/high assumptions remain one miner per owner, 1/10/16 owners and
0.25/1/4 active hours/day. The qualified generator potentials before and after
are respectively 142.5/570/2280 cobblestone, 6/24/96 coal and 1.5/6/24 raw iron
per owner-day. Three miners multiply both sides equally. Finite native ore
yield depends on the real terrain and is not priced from geometry counts.

Direct deltas are separately **0 Vault, 0 tokens, 0 player XP**. Resident skill
keeps its existing cap of 40. Building/tool prices and courier conservation
are unchanged. Current SELL remains a separate possible conversion; the
owner's planned SELL-off/contract transition is preserved. No unverified
sale price, new redemption flow or combined item/currency total is assumed.

Acceptance requires paid native work and actual walking after activation.
Teleport recovery does not establish walking. Source, tests, delivered JAR,
active runtime and native-client feel are recorded separately.
