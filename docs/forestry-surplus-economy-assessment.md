# Forestry family surplus admission

New PHYSICAL forestry actions pause at 512 raw-timber family items and resume at 256. These starting values are operator policy, not measured optima. The thresholds are source constants, not an added runtime configuration. All species share one city latch; a confirmed supported-species deficit permits that species. Existing selected work, paid multi-batch trees and historical receipts complete unchanged. Other professions, item/cargo ownership and material requirements remain intact.

Only complete/current observations are eligible: every indexed warehouse, town-hall, builder and production holder counts once, plus current plain worker bags once. A chest is captured as its own 27-slot half. Unpicked reservations stay in source stock; carried cargo is already in its bag. Custom stacks and inaccessible corpse holdings are excluded. Capture/input changes, unavailable holders, missing metadata or age over 15 seconds cannot start or release a surplus latch. A previously confirmed pause remains until complete fresh stock proves the resume threshold; current authored species demand can conservatively exempt only that species without claiming an unknown stock deficit. Coverage requires native loaded and cached-index membership for at most 256 distinct chunks covering active non-infrastructure bounds, controls and registered holders. Coverage is checked at capture start, finish and decision. Registered outside holders join the unique total and receive a separate aggregate event revision; ordinary courier validation does not require this full-city proof. Infrastructure bounds contribute no implicit holders, while their explicit registered holders or controls still count. The existing capture mode fallback also admits autonomous cities with null inventory.

The 512 stop value is eight normal stacks versus a barrel's 27 slots; the 256 hysteresis is four stacks, above the existing two-slot output headroom and 64-item courier batch. It does not reserve eight stacks per species. Paid trees can finish above the threshold; no hard cap, deletion or receipt rewriting occurs. The latch belongs to one supplies owner, is separated by city UUID and pruned when a city disappears. A process restart recalculates it from the stop threshold, so a previously paused city between 257 and 511 may admit new work again.

Species demand uses the current construction bill, valid accepted craft-job inputs and fixed inputs for staffed active non-carpentry production. Existing plain intermediates are consumed in one shared ledger before expanding pinned species recipes. Generic wood recipes do not invent a species preference; unsupported species recipes exempt only their exact species. The missing recipe is not invented and never replaced by an oak recipe. Unannounced native writes still rely on the next bounded periodic capture and the 15-second observation age; cached membership alone cannot detect a silent edit in a loaded chunk. The construction bill is conservative while an order remains active, so it can admit more timber than the next unpaid row needs.

Sensitivity assumes initial family stock 512, no active paid tree, no consumption and three-log oaks: 15/60/240 new trees per day yield 45/180/720 items before and zero while paused. This is not measured throughput or proof of renewable trees. Direct Vault, tokens and player XP remain separately zero. Resident skill opportunities decrease with skipped real cycles; earned skills and lifetime 40-point caps remain intact. Tools, saplings, native drops and recipes retain their per-action terms. Consumption or a confirmed species deficit resumes ordinary native work.
