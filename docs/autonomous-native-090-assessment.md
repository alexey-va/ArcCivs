These are hypothetical upper bounds, not measured income.

Normal economic delivery remains 3/6/9 FOOD or STONE at L1/L2/L3, after 60 seconds of observed work (120 seconds at hunger ≤ 6), real travel to the depot and the original unique economic receipt. Native subactions do not pay, train, advance the economic token or create inventory items. The L1 selected upper bounds stay 45/180/720 units per village-day in each stock unit and 315/5,040/30,240 per network-week; XP for the two independent residents stays 30/120/480 per village-day. L2/L3 stock ceilings remain 360/540 units per worker-hour before travel/sleep, versus L1 180. Ore replaces stone sessions and is not additive. Gaps, pending persistence, travel, night, pauses and offline time provide no work credit.

The existing paid Auto building now supplies its real scenery. These are world assets, not extra transferable production. Repeated repair can replace modified cells, so a finite original plan alone would not prevent extraction farming.

The exact Auto farm/mine domain therefore uses the existing normal-player read-only protection, with administrator RW retained. Authored crop growth is allowed outside pending receipt locks. Native writes create no drops; ordinary players cannot break/harvest/drop those scenery cells. Ordinary transferable-item output and direct Vault/tokens/player XP remain separately zero. Administrative removal is not a normal earning path. This is a precise worksite domain, not new protection of the entire settlement or public road approaches.

Courier interception changes availability, with current identity, surplus, nearby real positions and line of sight rechecked before pickup. It changes neither recipes nor transfer quotas and preserves sealed receipts. Native receipts and ordinary resource receipts remain distinct through recovery.

The landing repair retains the existing one-STONE price per worked column, finite stock, three-second action and next-building reserve. It stores a bounded local grade override through the paid receipt while historical captures and payments stay unchanged. Public road resale remains the separate [0.1.89 assessment](resident-roads-089-assessment.md). The captured spruce plank disk SELL price is 0.56 Vault; oak/dark-oak plank quotes are unknown, so no new all-plank cap is asserted. No additional shop price, infrastructure cap or money rate is changed. Current SELL remains current; SELL-off/contracts remains future.

Decision: retain the prior abstract rates and observed-work minimum, keep native presentation separate from transferable resources, and revalidate the exact receipt on replay. Live throughput, native-client movement and indirect public-road resale remain unmeasured. Delivery is authorized without restart, so delivery alone does not activate or verify these behaviors.
