# Organic settlement density proposal

This assessment covers an unactivated preview proposal. V1 in 0.1.69/0.1.70 uses eleven ordinary sites, an ordinary building cap of sixteen, and at most twelve residents. The preview does not reprice or rearrange accepted construction.

| Contract | Current | Proposed preview |

| Ordinary buildings | 11 | 28 |
| Houses | 3 | 20 |
| Existing service types | 8 | 8 |
| Autonomous hard cap | 16 | 32 |
| Player settlement hard cap | 16 | 16 |
| Resident cap | 12 | 12 |

One level-one house continues to cost 24 abstract wood and 12 abstract stone. Seventeen additional houses consume 408 wood and 204 stone once; the house-only target changes from 72/36 to 480/240 wood/stone. Food cost is zero. Bed capacity rises from nine to sixty, while the population cap and recruitment cadence remain unchanged. Extra capacity is not a grant of residents, tools, food or equipment.

Production counts, cycle quantities, stock limits and native-item redemption remain unchanged. Abstract village stock has no declared item or currency conversion. Direct deltas are separately zero Vault, zero premium tokens and zero player XP. The current SELL flow and planned SELL-to-contract transition remain unchanged. Empty template containers add capacity without granting stacks.

For one expansion each, assumed cohorts of one/five/ten villages spend 408/2,040/4,080 abstract wood and 204/1,020/2,040 stone. These are once-only scenarios, not measured daily or weekly activity. At the unchanged ideal L1 producer quantities, the additional bill requires at least 102 wood cycles and 68 stone cycles. Night, walking and capacity reduce actual throughput; no measured savings time or monetary return is claimed.

The final synthetic hillside export has 162 infrastructure pieces: 77 walls, 82 roads and three gates. At unchanged unit prices (wall 8 wood / 16 stone; road 0 / 8; gate 12 / 16), their once-only total is 652 abstract wood and 1,936 abstract stone. Together with twenty L1 houses this is 1,132 wood and 2,176 stone, excluding unchanged services. The prototype exceeds the unchanged production infrastructure cap of 96 by 66 pieces; it is not activated and does not change that cap. A different V1 terrain fixture is not treated as a paired economic baseline. Existing reservations and crash receipts retain exact accepted costs.

The separate V1 safe-site fallback and movement repair restore existing intended work without a nominal output multiplier. Fluids, foreign or occupied blocks and live protection remain exclusions. Any improvement in realized production is unmeasured.
