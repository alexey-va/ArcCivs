# ArcCivs 0.1.18 entrance preparation

The requested entrance preparation grants at most one placed DIRT per explicit
preparation and clears at most two headroom blocks without drops. Existing solid
support stays. A durable pending-control receipt owns these changes; completed
receipts do not regenerate filler on a timer. Normal schematic material costs,
free control barrels, production rates, recruitment and starter grants are unchanged.

[Calculation](placement-economy-assessment.json) and
[result](placement-economy-assessment-result.json) keep item units separate from
money. Assumed 1/4/16 requests per owner-day and 1/10/16 participating owners yield
at most 1/4/16 new DIRT per owner-day and 7/280/1792 per network-week. These are
scenarios, not measured activity or hard limits. Deletion/refounding and explicit
new preparations can issue more over time; the concurrent-building limit is not
a lifetime cap. No new restriction is introduced for this small filler grant.

Direct deltas are separately zero Vault, tokens and XP. Live Spawn shop file
DIRT sell price is 1 Vault; the 168-hour ledger has 2,392 DIRT sold for 2,261.31
Vault across five transactions by two players. The conditional all-recovered,
all-sold scenario therefore adds at most 1/4/16 Vault per owner-day and
7/280/1792 per network-week at that file price, before taxes or restrictions.
This is indirect resale exposure, not an ArcCivs payout or observed player income.
SELL remains active; the planned SELL-to-contract transition remains future and
no replacement DIRT contract is assumed. No savings or progression price changes.

Verification covers partial receipt recovery, no drops, no repeat after retirement,
bedrock, inventory data and Lands permissions. Source tests, runtime activation
and native-client acceptance are separate evidence.
