# Settlement plan board: balance

0.1.84 adds one ordinary, single-level PLAN_BOARD. It uses one of the existing sixteen ordinary building slots; infrastructure remains capped at 96. The board adds no resident job, housing, output, recurring training or abstract-stock capacity. Ordinary builders retain their existing +1 BUILDER_HUT skill XP per accepted completed delivery/session, capped at 40; placing a native batch or finishing the building adds no separate training. This is resident skill, distinct from player XP. It is not automatically inserted into the autonomous progression profile. Normal owner/admin permissions and protected building bounds remain authoritative.

An accepted autonomous board costs **12 WOOD and 8 STONE**, with no FOOD cost or refund. Physical settlements reserve the exact native schematic materials through existing construction receipts rather than paying this abstract price as well. The body contains 6 stone bricks, 18 stripped spruce logs, 15 smooth sandstone, 14 dark oak stairs and 7 dark oak slabs: sixty solid blocks. The existing SITE-control mechanism immediately grants or reuses one barrel before body construction, with **27 physical inventory slots**; the physical body material reservation happens later. This timing is unchanged. This is distinct from zero additional abstract-stock capacity. The schematic additionally clears 55 aboveground AIR cells through existing protected construction and leaves ten ground gaps untouched. AIR adds no material charge or item grant; protection and nonempty-container guards remain unchanged. Client-only map pieces are not transferable items or native MapView IDs.

Before, this building was unavailable. Low/base/high stress assumptions are 1/4/8 accepted boards per owner-day and 1/10/16 participating owners, not observed activity. The added daily sink is 12/48/96 WOOD and separately 8/32/64 STONE per owner. Weekly network sinks are 84/3360/10752 WOOD and 56/2240/7168 STONE. Direct Vault, tokens and player XP are separately zero. One level is the complete progression price; no additional upgrade or recurring bonus is introduced.

Twelve WOOD corresponds to four net level-1 forester completions at three WOOD each, or two level-2 completions at seven; eight STONE needs three level-1 quarry deliveries at three or two level-2 deliveries at six. Travel, growth, hunger, night and storage limits remain part of the real cadence. These are resource requirements, not a claimed elapsed or monetary saving time.

Autonomous construction is an existing administrative abstract-stock-to-world bridge. A board adds at most 61 normal world blocks per accepted paid order, not 61 logs or 61 Vault. Repeated removal and rebuilding pay again and return no abstract refund; physical blocks remain under the existing deletion behavior. Salvage still needs existing editor access and suitable tools. No new unprivileged right to place or dismantle autonomous buildings is granted. In physical cities the body uses prepaid native materials; the standard SITE barrel remains the existing separate control grant. Reading or updating the packet map adds no item grant. The informational packet scene cannot be picked into an inventory.

Current ledger and the SELL/contract distinction are recorded in the same task's [forester assessment](forester-care-assessment.md). Existing mixed-material SELL quotes were not measured here and are not invented. SELL remains current until separately disabled; selling one salvaged item and submitting that same item to a contract are alternatives. No shop price, budget, payout, yield, cap or boost changes. The selected price funds a small informational decoration through the existing construction mechanism; neither reading it nor updating its map pays a reward.

The [input](plan-board-economy-assessment.json) and [calculator result](plan-board-economy-assessment-result.json) separate WOOD, STONE, world blocks and monetary units. Source preview, JAR delivery, activation and native-client appearance remain separate evidence.
