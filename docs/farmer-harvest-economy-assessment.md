# Farmer harvest, 0.1.34

The owner requested removing baking from the farmer. Before, all farm levels could consume three wheat and produce one bread per native job. After, no new baking jobs are created; farms harvest wheat, carrots or potatoes. A stored BREAD selection remains loadable and resolves to wheat harvesting. Previously accepted receipts retain their paid inputs and outputs, including one pending bread job; existing bread remains usable food. No miller or baker is introduced.

Low/base/high assume 1/10/16 cities, one farmer active 1/2/4 hours daily and 1/4/16 bakes per minute before this change. That removes 60/480/3840 automated bread and 180/1440/11520 wheat consumption per city-day, with network-week amounts evaluated in the JSON. These are separate item units, not monetary emissions. The former fed ceiling was 2400 baking actions per hour (1200 hungry), constrained by available wheat, travel and persistence. Farm action duration, native growth, drops, worker limits and hunger costs do not change.

Direct Vault and token mint/burn and player XP delta are zero. Players can still craft ordinary bread themselves; therefore this removes an automated profession recipe, not access to food or a currency sink. Existing SELL remains a possible redemption path for crops; the planned contract-only economy is not active by assumption. Net wheat-versus-bread sale income is unknown, so no guessed price or accumulation time is presented. No progression price is changed, and no simultaneous full-time occupations are added together.
