# Builder upkeep of accepted autonomous roads

While no construction is active, an autonomous builder inspects saved approaches and completed ROAD routes. Turning stair flights reserve a full flat wooden landing, with no full-block rise onto the outgoing flight. New road construction and maintenance share the same finish: DIRT_PATH flats and dry bottom wooden stairs, using oak, spruce or dark oak from the existing settlement style. Accepted old construction plans and maintenance receipts keep their saved bytes, amounts and heights; the legacy cobble finish remains a supported recovery state.

Maintenance expands the retained centerline through the existing three-wide road-profile geometry. Conflicting retained grades and every building's complete maximum-level footprint, controls, fields and irrigation stay protected. One-level buildings reserve only their real catalog level. A shoulder without bounded safe support can remain unmodified; this is not a promise of full width at every cliff. Existing paid route captures are not deleted or rewritten. Version 0.1.90 may add a separate exact route override for a bounded turn landing: the same X/Z route and endpoints, with only one or two local feet heights lowered by one. The first paid upkeep receipt captures both original and corrected routes; replay restores that same override without a second charge. Retained routes still protect crossings and building access.

Short straight humps may also be lowered to their equal-height approaches: at most nine raised columns, at most three blocks deep. Matching retained approaches of gates and walls share the same override receipt; their authored building blocks and transit remain unchanged. A crossing at another grade still blocks the cut. Current native preflight must prove the complete three-wide cut before its first receipt.

New autonomous designs and queued activation wait for an admitted upkeep job and a bounded native pass of the lowered footprint. Saving the first route override does not mean the remaining columns have been cut. The existing scan owner checks those columns after the network reload, using the same aggregate budget. Healthy, unavailable and unrepairable candidates retire from that admission pass; unrelated ordinary wear cannot indefinitely hold the next building order. A complete current supply observation proving no available or carried cobblestone also releases admission, so upkeep cannot prevent building its missing production source. This changes priority, never the payment or grant quantity.

Each job handles one eligible column with at most five exact changes: one floor and up to four AIR headroom cuts. Natural banks may be opened only to known air within that bound, without fluid, containers, attachments, protected blocks or unsupported foreign construction. Cut material creates no drop. Grass and ordinary/large ferns are journalled completely; flowers and unsupported decoration are preserved by skipping the column. A healthy floor can receive clearance-only work without a no-op floor entry. Native validation and recovery recheck the real floor, support, body envelope, neighbouring traversable connections, owner protection and occupancy.

Supported floors require full native support or a known dry road stair immediately below, touching the new floor's bottom. Other partial blocks, unknown holes, liquids and tiles remain excluded. Workers must reach a real standing point and a visible actually changed focus; a hidden bank does not license remote work through a wall. Existing native work/collision checks apply.

One actually worked column costs **one native COBBLESTONE** in physical storage mode, including a clearance-only job. Legacy closed-stock Auto settlements retain their saved **one internal STONE** debit and next-goal reserve. Healthy columns cost zero. The existing three-second work, finite supplies, aggregate scan budget and durable receipt remain unchanged. No cut drop, refund, training or monetary reward is added. Repeated recovery uses the same receipt, preventing a second payment or work credit.

Accepted healthy wooden stairs are registered with the optional Trails provider through the same bounded native probe budget. Registration changes metadata only. Trails 2.3.25 uses an explicit wooden-base speed when configured, otherwise the configured cobblestone stage; unregistered stairs, upper stairs and waterlogged stairs retain their ordinary behavior. Repeated registration does not advance wear or refresh decay activity.
