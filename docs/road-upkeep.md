# Builder upkeep of accepted autonomous roads

After accepted construction and its queue, an autonomous builder inspects saved approaches and completed ROAD routes. Turning stair flights reserve a full flat wooden landing, with no full-block rise onto the outgoing flight. New road construction and maintenance share the same finish: DIRT_PATH flats and dry bottom wooden stairs, using oak, spruce or dark oak from the existing settlement style. Accepted old construction plans and maintenance receipts keep their saved bytes, amounts and heights; the legacy cobble finish remains a supported recovery state.

Maintenance expands the retained centerline through the existing three-wide road-profile geometry. Conflicting retained grades and every building's complete maximum-level footprint, controls, fields and irrigation stay protected. One-level buildings reserve only their real catalog level. A shoulder without bounded safe support can remain unmodified; this is not a promise of full width at every cliff. Existing paid route captures are not deleted or rewritten. Version 0.1.90 may add a separate exact route override for a bounded turn landing: the same X/Z route and endpoints, with only one or two local feet heights lowered by one. The first paid upkeep receipt captures both original and corrected routes; replay restores that same override without a second charge. Retained routes still protect crossings and building access.

Each job handles one eligible column with at most five exact changes: one floor and up to four AIR headroom cuts. Natural banks may be opened only to known air within that bound, without fluid, containers, attachments, protected blocks or unsupported foreign construction. Cut material creates no drop. Grass and ordinary/large ferns are journalled completely; flowers and unsupported decoration are preserved by skipping the column. A healthy floor can receive clearance-only work without a no-op floor entry. Native validation and recovery recheck the real floor, support, body envelope, neighbouring traversable connections, owner protection and occupancy.

Supported floors require full native support or a known dry road stair immediately below, touching the new floor's bottom. Other partial blocks, unknown holes, liquids and tiles remain excluded. Workers must reach a real standing point and a visible actually changed focus; a hidden bank does not license remote work through a wall. Existing native work/collision checks apply.

One actually worked column costs **one internal STONE**, including a clearance-only job; healthy columns cost zero. The next ordinary goal retains its stone reserve. The existing three-second work, finite stock, aggregate scan budget and durable native receipt remain unchanged. No native inventory grant, cut drop, refund, training or monetary reward is added. Repeated recovery uses the same receipt, preventing a second payment or work credit.
