# Resident food: physical ownership and paid Auto buffer

Verified source base: ArcCivs `6ddb49b` plus the existing species and resident-life extraction deltas. This change keeps the normal adult hunger clock at one point per observed awake minute and bread at six restored hunger per consumed item. Sleeping/absent time, work rewards, production rates, prices, recruitment and currency APIs are unchanged.

Auto settlements retain `inventory = null` and their existing abstract production/construction owner. Food is the narrow new item bridge: one existing `stock.food` becomes one ordinary native `BREAD`, with the debit and exact native slot credit in a single typed receipt. The public buffer targets four bread in a reachable staffed tavern, otherwise an active warehouse, otherwise the hall. Full, unloaded or unreachable holders leave the reserve untouched or use an honest fallback. Existing native contents are retained; four is the refill target of the selected holder, not a global cap on player-supplied food or older fallback holders.

The buffer is real between operations. A materialization receipt first persists the native before-state, journals the immutable intent, applies the chest credit, persists and verifies its native after-state, publishes the stock debit, then retires the journal. A later pickup is a separate receipt that debits actual container slots and credits the resident's own bag. The existing per-world five-second receipt admission window separates these operations. It does not conceal a repeated food grant. Empty FOOD cannot materialize bread; empty native slots cannot grant carried food; recovery reuses the exact accepted receipt and UUID.

Residents with usable carried food eat on their supported current position. Empty bags cause a trip to the closest stocked permitted native holder among public stores, their own home and their assigned workplace. Arbitrary other homes and cross-city targets are rejected. Pickup moves up to the existing four-item supply batch. Carried plain food restores hunger only when the observed eating session completes. Severe hunger batches the existing necessary items into one session; each item is still consumed exactly once, and each contributes its established nutrition. Interrupted sessions consume none. The existing sixteen-bread personal courier/home reserve is retained; newly supported plain food already in the bag also has a sixteen-item personal reserve per material, in addition to actual profession ingredients. Supply planning and the final cargo pickup both enforce that reserve, so a courier cannot take the resident's meal after an older plan was formed.

Safe ordinary foods are captured once on Main through Paper 1.21.11 `ItemStack.getData(FOOD)` and `CONSUMABLE`. New foods use native nutrition and consume duration; bread preserves the established +6 rule. The catalog excludes native effects, unhandled bowl/bottle remainder and non-EAT consumption. Actual pickup and meals exclude custom item components, equipped items, shipment-reserved amounts and profession ingredient reserves. Apple has no abstract FOOD charge: it is an existing physical item that moves chest → resident → consumption. Vanilla prototype values are missing in MockBukkit, so the component adapter has a narrow mocked read seam; exact production defaults remain a live Paper readback requirement.

The indirect economic change is explicit: native bread can be moved under existing inventory permissions and potentially sold later. No sale price or measured sale volume is assumed. Current SELL and the intended SELL-to-contract transition remain separate. At the receipt cadence alone, the theoretical transfer ceiling is four items per five seconds, or 2,880 native bread per world-hour, shared by cities and still constrained by earned FOOD, finite stock, actual room, travel and other receipts. This is a bound, not observed production or income. Availability changes can leave previously paid bread in an older fallback holder; no native items are erased to enforce a cosmetic global count.

The [calculation input](resident-food-economy-assessment-20261028.json) and [validated result](resident-food-economy-assessment-result-20261028.json) keep FOOD, bread, apples, Vault, tokens and player XP separate. Illustrative adult bread-only demand is 10 bread per resident-hour; the apple-only alternative is 15 apples per resident-hour at native nutrition four. These are alternative diets, not additive earning opportunities. Direct changes are zero Vault, zero tokens and zero player XP. There is no new monetary price or progression cost.

Verification includes red/green carried-meal regressions, exact native chest/bag conservation, remaining-stock and refill bounds, a stocked reserve between operations, nearest permitted source and fallback, named/work-reserved exclusions, apple and bread consumption, before/after disk-fence failures, partial apply, same-ID recovery, replay rejection, legacy collected/store tests, native route elevation and eating interruption/retirement. The hand is equipped with the actual food before native item use starts; use stops before the original tool is restored. Packet/component tests do not establish client appearance, sound perception, activation or delivery.
